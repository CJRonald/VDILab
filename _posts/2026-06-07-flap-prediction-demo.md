A second public demo classifies free-flap circulation from a photograph of the flap region, available on [Hugging Face Spaces](https://huggingface.co/spaces/CJRonald/flap-prediction-demo).
An earlier version was retired after we found data leakage across patients; the current model is trained and evaluated with patient-level splits.
