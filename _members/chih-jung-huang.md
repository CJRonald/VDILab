Plastic surgeon at the Department of Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery, Chang Gung Memorial Hospital, and PhD student at the Institute of Biomedical Informatics, National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University.
His work connects burn intensive care, rhinoplasty and craniofacial surgery with data science — building quantitative measurement methods and clinical AI models that can be used at the bedside.
